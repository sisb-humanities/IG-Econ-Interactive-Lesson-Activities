Thailand

One decision in New Delhi. One farmer in Suphan Buri.

The rice in Suphan Buri did not change. Its price did – twice.

July 2023India, the world's biggest rice exporter, bans exports of most white rice. Buyers in the Philippines, Indonesia and Africa look for rice somewhere else.
August 2023Thai white rice (5% broken) reaches about US$648 per tonne – the highest price since 2008.
28 September 2024India lifts the ban. The price Thai farmers get for paddy falls from about ฿10,000 to ฿8,000–9,000 per tonne.

Think first

For the market for Thai rice, what did India's ban in 2023 do?

And in September 2024, when India lifted the ban?

Predict: the day after the ban, buyers wanted much more Thai rice than exporters had ready. What happened first?

The big idea of this topic

Shift → gap → price moves → new equilibrium

A change in a non-price factor shifts demand or supply. At the old price there is now a shortage or a surplus. The price moves, buyers and sellers move along the curves, and the gap closes at a new equilibrium.
