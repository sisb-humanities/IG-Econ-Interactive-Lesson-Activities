1 · Deficit or surplus?

Balance = revenue − spending. A negative answer is a deficit; a positive answer is a surplus.

The rule A direct tax is charged on income or wealth — the person or firm charged must pay it themselves. An indirect tax is charged on spending — the firm pays it to the government but can pass some of it on to the customer in a higher price.
