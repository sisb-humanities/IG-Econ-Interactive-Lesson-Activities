Why did Labubu prices crash?

After Lisa of BLACKPINK was seen with one, Labubu toys sold out across Thailand and resale prices shot far above the shop price. Then, in 2025, Pop Mart said it had raised plush toy production about ten times, to around 30 million a month. Resale prices fell fast. One Thai reseller told Reuters that prices in the Thai resale market were dropping extremely quickly.

Observation or inference?

An observation is something that was measured or reported. An inference is an explanation someone has worked out from the evidence. Economists keep them apart.

Multiple choice

Build the 4-mark answer

Explain, with a relevant example, how a shortage is removed in a market system. [4]

Tap the sentences in the right order: Define → Example → Explain → Develop.

A shortage means the quantity demanded is greater than the quantity supplied at a given price. For example, during Songkran week almost every hotel room in Phuket is booked and many tourists cannot find a room. This happens because tourists compete for the few rooms left, so hotels raise their prices. Therefore, the higher price makes some tourists stay fewer nights or go elsewhere, while more homestays offer rooms, until the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied.
Why this scores well: one mark each for a precise definition, a real Thai example, a “because” explanation, and an outcome that answers the question (the shortage is removed).
