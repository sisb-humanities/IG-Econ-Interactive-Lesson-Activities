Puzzle 1 · Thailand, 2023

One country, two worlds?

In 2023 the value of output per person in Bangkok was about 676,000 baht. In the Northeast (Isan) it was about 99,000 baht. The average for the whole of Thailand was 256,345 baht.

Puzzle 2 · Viet Nam and Indonesia, 2023

Similar incomes, different lives

Income per head in Indonesia was $13,700; in Viet Nam it was $13,033. But people in Viet Nam live 74.6 years on average (Indonesia: 71.1), and children can expect 15.5 years of school (Indonesia: 13.3).

So what do the puzzles show?

Gross provincial product (output) per person by region, 2023, thousand baht. Source: NESDC, Statistical Yearbook Thailand 2025.

Puzzle 1: an average such as GDP per head hides big differences between regions and between people. The Thai average is far below Bangkok and far above the Northeast.

Puzzle 2: income is not everything. The Human Development Index (HDI) adds health and education: Viet Nam scores 0.766, Indonesia 0.728.

Why is it useful to know about this?

Your futureWhere will you study, work and live? Comparing living standards helps you judge the choices.
Your countryThailand is 76th of 193 countries on the HDI, just below “very high”. What would move it up?
FairnessWhy is Bangkok so far ahead of Isan? You need this to judge handouts, taxes and spending on regions.

Why real GDP per head can mislead

ProblemWhat it meansThai example
It is an averageExtra income may go to a few people; many get nothing extraBangkok output per person is almost 7 times the Northeast’s
Not all output makes life betterSpending on pollution, crime or illness raises GDP but people are not better offAir purifiers, masks and asthma treatment in the PM2.5 season
Unrecorded outputInformal (undeclared) work and unpaid work at home are missed, so GDP may understate living standards52.7% of Thai workers were informal in 2024

QualityBetter quality at the same price is not shown, so GDP may understate gainsA phone at the same price with a much better camera
