Thailand · Nonthaburi

A train line with empty seats

The MRT Purple Line runs through Nonthaburi, from Khlong Bang Phai to Tao Poon.

August 2016The Purple Line opens. The target is at least 100,000 passengers a day. Reality: about 20,000 a day.
1 September 2016The operator cuts the fare. The top fare falls from 42 baht to 29 baht: a cut of about 31%.
After the cutAbout 22,000 passengers a day: up by 10%.

Source: Bangkok Post. The top fare stands in for the typical fare to keep the maths simple.

Think first

The fare is cut by 31%. What do you expect to happen to the number of riders?

Riders went from 20,000 to 22,000 (+10%). The fare went down by 31%. Which change was bigger?

So how responsive were the riders to the fall in price?

Now it has a name

Economists divide the two percentages. This is price elasticity of demand (PED).

PED = %Δ quantity demanded ÷ %Δ price = (+10) ÷ (−31) = −0.32

Ignoring the minus sign, 0.32 is less than 1. Quantity changed by a smaller percentage than price, so demand was price inelastic.
