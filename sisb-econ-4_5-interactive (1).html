Think first: two Thai stories

Both stories are about how much Thailand produced. Answer the two questions under each story.

Story 1 · 2022

A record GDP in baht

In 2022 Thailand’s GDP, measured in baht at that year’s prices, rose by 7.4%. It was the biggest rise for years. But prices in Thailand also rose quickly that year: inflation was about 6%.

Story 2 · 2020

The tourists disappear

In 2019 Thailand had 39.8 million foreign tourists. In April 2020 COVID-19 travel bans began, and the year ended with only 6.7 million. Hotels in Phuket and Pattaya closed.

So what do the stories show?

Story 1: nominal GDP is not output. Nominal GDP rose 7.4%, but most of that was higher prices. Real GDP, which removes inflation, rose only 2.6%. Economic growth is an increase in real GDP.

Story 2: a recession. Real GDP fell in three quarters in a row. A recession is a fall in real GDP over six months or more (two quarters in a row). Here a fall in total demand (tourists’ spending) caused it.
