TH The problem in the world

Why are people queuing for a toy?

In 2024, Lisa from BLACKPINK posted photos of a small toy monster called Labubu. Within weeks it became one of the most wanted products in Thailand.

Pop Mart sells Labubu ‘blind boxes’ in its shops at a fixed price of a few hundred baht. Fans queued for hours outside Pop Mart stores in Bangkok malls. Some stores limited how many boxes each customer could buy, and new stock sold out within minutes.

On resale websites, rare Labubu figures sold for many times the shop price.
